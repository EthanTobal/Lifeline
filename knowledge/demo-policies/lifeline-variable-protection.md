# LifeLine Variable Protection

> **HACKATHON DEMO — FICTIONAL PRODUCT**
>
> This is not an actual Lincoln Financial policy, quote, premium, guarantee, investment recommendation, or insurance recommendation.

## Product type
Variable universal life (VUL) demo

## Demo purpose
Illustrates longer-term life-insurance protection with cash value that may fluctuate based on investment options.

## Illustrative characteristics

| Feature | Demo characteristic |
|---|---|
| Coverage period | Longer-term / lifetime potential |
| Premium structure | Flexible |
| Death benefit | May vary based on policy structure |
| Cash-value growth | Potential growth based on investment options |
| Market exposure | Greater exposure to market movement |
| Primary tradeoff | Greater growth potential can also involve greater variability, expenses, and risk |

## Situations it can illustrate
- The user wants to understand permanent coverage with market-linked investment exposure.
- The user asks how VUL differs from IUL.
- The user wants to understand the relationship between potential growth and market risk.

## Tradeoffs LifeLine may explain
The demo structure can illustrate greater growth potential alongside greater exposure to market movement. Cash value can fluctuate and growth is not guaranteed.
