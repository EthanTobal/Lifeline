# LifeLine Term 30

> **HACKATHON DEMO — FICTIONAL PRODUCT**
>
> This is not an actual Lincoln Financial policy, quote, premium, or recommendation.

## Product type
Term life insurance

## Demo purpose
Illustrates straightforward protection for a longer defined period of 30 years. It can be used in the demo when a user's time-bound obligations extend further into the future.

## Illustrative characteristics

| Feature | Demo characteristic |
|---|---|
| Coverage period | 30 years |
| Premium structure | Fixed during the demo term |
| Death benefit | Fixed |
| Cash-value growth | None |
| Relative cost framing | Generally lower than permanent coverage |
| Primary tradeoff | Longer temporary protection, but coverage still ends and no cash value accumulates |

## Situations it can illustrate
- Younger dependents may require support for many years.
- A mortgage has a long remaining term.
- The user wants to compare different temporary coverage periods.
- The user has long-duration but still time-bound obligations.

## Tradeoffs LifeLine may explain
Compared with a shorter term example, a longer term keeps the illustrative protection period in place longer. It remains temporary coverage and does not provide cash-value growth.
