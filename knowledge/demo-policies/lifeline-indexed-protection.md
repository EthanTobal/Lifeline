# LifeLine Indexed Protection

> **HACKATHON DEMO — FICTIONAL PRODUCT**
>
> This is not an actual Lincoln Financial policy, quote, premium, guarantee, or recommendation.

## Product type
Indexed universal life (IUL) demo

## Demo purpose
Illustrates longer-term life-insurance protection with potential cash-value growth tied to a market index.

## Illustrative characteristics

| Feature | Demo characteristic |
|---|---|
| Coverage period | Longer-term / lifetime potential |
| Premium structure | Flexible |
| Death benefit | May vary based on policy structure |
| Cash-value growth | Potential growth linked to an index |
| Market framing | Index-linked; not direct investment in the index |
| Primary tradeoff | More complexity and cash-value potential versus straightforward term protection |

## Situations it can illustrate
- The user wants to understand protection intended to remain in place longer.
- The user asks about potential cash value in addition to a death benefit.
- The user wants to compare term insurance with a permanent insurance structure.
- The user wants to understand index-linked growth concepts.

## Tradeoffs LifeLine may explain
This demo structure adds potential cash-value features and greater flexibility compared with term insurance, while also introducing more complexity. Cash-value growth should never be described as guaranteed.
