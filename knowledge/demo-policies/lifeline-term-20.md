# LifeLine Term 20

> **HACKATHON DEMO — FICTIONAL PRODUCT**
>
> This is not an actual Lincoln Financial policy, quote, premium, or recommendation.

## Product type
Term life insurance

## Demo purpose
Illustrates straightforward protection for a defined 20-year period. It is intended for scenarios involving time-bound financial responsibilities such as income replacement, a mortgage, debts, or supporting dependents while they are younger.

## Illustrative characteristics

| Feature | Demo characteristic |
|---|---|
| Coverage period | 20 years |
| Premium structure | Fixed during the demo term |
| Death benefit | Fixed |
| Cash-value growth | None |
| Relative cost framing | Generally lower than permanent coverage |
| Primary tradeoff | Coverage is temporary and does not build cash value |

## Situations it can illustrate
- A family has dependents who rely on the insured person's income.
- A mortgage or other obligation is expected to decline over time.
- The user wants to explore protection during a defined period.
- Affordability is an important consideration.

## Tradeoffs LifeLine may explain
A defined term can align with temporary obligations and may have a lower relative cost than permanent coverage. The tradeoff is that coverage ends after the defined period and the policy does not build cash value.
