#### Different types of life insurance for different financial goals

#### Term life insurance

- **Coverage:** Limited term
- **Premium payments:** Fixed
- **Tax-free death benefit:** Fixed
- **Cost:** Generally lower
- **Cash value growth potential:** No

#### Indexed universal life

- **Coverage:** Varies
- **Premium payments:** Flexible
- **Tax-free death benefit:** Varies
- **Cost:** Varies
- **Cash value growth potential:** Yes

#### Variable universal life

- **Coverage:** Lifetime potential
- **Premium payments:** Flexible
- **Tax-free death benefit:** Varies
- **Cost:** Generally higher than other types of insurance
- **Cash value growth potential:** Yes 1
